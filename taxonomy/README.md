# Skill taxonomy

`skills.yaml` is generated by `backend/scripts/build_taxonomy.py`; edit `aliases.yaml`, not
`skills.yaml`.

Source: the O*NET® 30.2 Database "Technology Skills" file, Hot Technologies only
(`onet_hot_tech.txt`), by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training
Administration (USDOL/ETA), used under the
[CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). O*NET® is a trademark of
USDOL/ETA. Shortlist has modified the information: it keeps engineering categories only,
drops non-engineering entries, renames entries to the names job postings use, and adds
skills and aliases. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.
